## Tab by tab

### 1. Steel discs: Guangzhou Jiangy on Alibaba

- Search Alibaba for the supplier **Guangzhou Jiangy** and their brushed 430 stainless steel disc listing.
- If Liam is already signed in, his Messages may show the earlier conversation with them. Open that thread in a tab, but don't type anything.
- **Spec:** 430 stainless steel (must be magnetic, not 304 or 316), 1.5 mm thick, brushed finish, **266.7 mm diameter**, quantity 10. The earlier quote was A$4.75 a disc for 10.
- **Draft message for Liam:** ask them to re-confirm the 10-disc price, quote shipping to Melbourne, VIC 3000, and quote UV printing one sample disc with a supplied PDF.

### 2. Vinyl ring decals: Sticker Mule

- Open Sticker Mule's die-cut stickers page (stickermule.com, Australian site if offered).
- Set the size to about **10.5 in (267 mm)** across, die-cut, quantity 10. If 10 isn't allowed, note the smallest quantity and its price.
- Liam will upload `physical/board_art/tau_board_267_slate_RGB.pdf` or the dojo version himself, so stop before the upload step.
- Also note whether Sticker Mule offers a thinner or matte vinyl. The decal has to stay thin (about 0.1–0.2 mm) so the magnets still grip.

### 3. PETG filament: an Australian seller

- Find 1 kg of 1.75 mm PETG from an Australian seller with fast delivery to Melbourne, for example Amazon AU or a local 3D printing store.
- Black or a neutral colour. Pick a listing with good reviews, around A$30.

### 4. Magnets

- Find **2 mm × 2 mm N48** (or N52) neodymium disc magnets, at least 100 pieces.
- Prefer an Australian eBay or Amazon AU seller if one is near A$15. Otherwise use AliExpress, and note its delivery time, which can be about a month.

### 5. Spray paint: Bunnings

- Open Bunnings (bunnings.com.au) for a red and a blue spray paint that works on plastic, plus a plastic primer.
- Note the prices and whether the nearest Melbourne store has them in stock, if the site shows that without signing in.

### 6. QR rules cards: Vistaprint Australia

- Open Vistaprint AU's standard business cards page and select the smallest quantity.
- Liam uploads the design, so stop before uploading. Note the price, including delivery if it's shown.

### 7. Card stock: Officeworks

- Open Officeworks (officeworks.com.au) for heavy card stock, 300 gsm or more, in A3 if available, otherwise A4.
- Each box is roughly a 300 mm square base, so note the sheet size and how many sheets a pack has.

### 8. Outer mailers: Australia Post or Officeworks

- Find a mailing box that fits a roughly 300 × 300 mm flat box about 50 mm deep.
- Two are needed. Note the size and price.

### 9. Freight for the discs

- There's nothing to open for this. It's covered by the message draft in item 1.

### 10. Postage: Australia Post calculator

- Open the Australia Post international postage calculator.
- Price a 1.5 kg parcel of about 32 × 32 × 6 cm from Melbourne 3000 to Canada (Ontario) and to the United States, using standard and express.

### Optional: a print quote for the base

- Open JLC3DP's 3D printing quote page (jlc3dp.com) and leave it there. Liam will upload `physical/out_cad/tau_rim_267_full.stl` himself to get a price for having the base printed.
