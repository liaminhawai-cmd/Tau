# The dead-depth field: design and build plan

A depth-to-loss field over Tau positions -- dead in 1, in 2, in 3 ... -- built outward from certified dead positions by two operations that alternate: *all arcs inside* (the side to move) and *unwind* (the other side), with the contact law (nn/contact-law.js) entering only where an arc touches. Three independent designs were drawn up, judged by three lenses and synthesised; this file is the synthesis, kept with the code it specifies (nn/forced-win.js).

## Chosen design and grafts

TUBED FAMILY GRAPH (TFG), chosen by two of three judges, built as a set of additions to /home/user/Tau/nn/forced-win.js (no new module in v1), with these grafts, each settled by reading the code:

1. REACH ENVELOPES (from JBT, both TFG judges asked for it). The victim's swing limit is a function of the victim's 3 coordinates only (swingLimit's own comment, index.html:3402 pins only the mover). Every dead certificate over a region (the star at level 2, the child points at level 3) sweeps each reply family to reachEnvelope(...).hi, the max over a 5x5x5 grid of the victim's box plus a 3x finite-difference allowance plus ONE SUBSTEP, and refuses the box when neighbouring grid points stop on different events (limitReason + line ids + foot). This closes the silent domain gap that certifyDeadBox has today (each sample uses its own limit).

2. UNCAPPED LIMITS (from RFL). index.html:3273 AI_STEP_RAD = 3 deg and :3402 simMoveToLimit stops at AI_SAFETY_CAP_RAD = 170 deg; applySwing (:3095) stops only at 'selfoff' or 'cross'. A human reply is uncapped, so limitAt() loops applySwing to 2*pi. Cost measured today at ndpxhts24: 13-408 ms per arm vs 9-357 ms for simMoveToLimit, i.e. the same, because these arms hit their limit at 3-110 deg.

3. SUBSTEP PHASE (from JBT's PHASE_TOL, made mandatory). applySwing splits dA into ceil(|dA|/0.4deg) EQUAL substeps, so the ladder's phase depends on the caller: a 3-degree observation step gives 0.375-degree substeps, a 1-degree step 0.333, a human drag anything. Measured today: limits from 3-degree vs 1-degree steps differ by 0.1-0.3 deg on 9 of 12 arms at ndpxhts24. The law's record is on a fixed 0.4-degree ladder. So (a) every reach envelope adds one substep (0.4 deg) to hi and subtracts one from lo, and (b) PHASE_TOL, the p100 difference between the law's prefix record and the engine stopped at random angles over 200 recorded contacts, is measured FIRST (--phase-tol, ~3 min) and added to every pushed-landing radius. Nothing at level 3 is trusted before that number exists.

4. WITNESS-AMPLIFIED GAP LENGTH (judges 2 and 3). Along a reply arm the child position C(t1) = witness applied to landing L(t1). The Lipschitz floor must be applied to the CHILD's path length, not the victim's: gapU_child = 2R*dt1 (victim, exact rigid rotation) + 3*attPath (the pushed attacker's recorded path over [A,B], amplified by the witness's rigid map, whose L1 Lipschitz constant is exactly 3: hub moves <= |dxy| + 2R|drot|, rot moves |drot|) + 2R*dsigma (the interpolated witness stop). For a contact-free reply attPath = 0 and the floor is the same 0.81u per 2 deg as today; for a pushing reply it is 3x the push, which is what forces the subdivision TFG's risk 3 already budgets for.

5. METRIC = L1 PER PIECE (judge 3's sqrt(6) objection). d(p,q) = sum over pieces of (|dxy|_2 + R*|drot|), R = 23.095. With an L1 metric, |f(x)-f(y)| <= (max axis partial) * ||x-y||_1, so the star's max-over-axes finite difference is a valid Lipschitz constant (under the codebase's standing heuristic: true partial <= 3x observed, floor 1). A star certifies an L1 ball, not a max-metric ball. It is smaller (radius eps = min(h, W/lip) shared between the two pieces) but it is the region the evidence supports.

6. PROVABLE ARC RADII (from JBT, replacing TFG's '(minGap - MIND)/4'). The tube gap between rigid bodies is 1-Lipschitz per piece in the L1 metric (no point of a tripod is farther than R from its hub) and (R*sqrt(3) = 40u/rad)-Lipschitz in the swing angle (every point of the swinging piece is within R*sqrt(3) of the pivot foot). So an arc position perturbed by eps (L1, both pieces together) with the sweep sampled every 0.5 deg stays contact-free while minGap - MIND > eps + 40u * 0.25 deg = eps + 0.17u. A proof, no sampling.

7. KO (from JBT, scoped by the code). koViolation (index.html:3170) requires !koContactThisTurn(): a push wipes the history, so only a CONTACT-FREE witness can be ko-banned, and only if the child position was on the board earlier in the game. Arc nodes are contact-free by definition and carry a sigma interval [s0,s1] >= koDeg = 2 deg, which koLegalizePlan's 2-degree nudge cannot leave. A contact-free level-3 witness at a landing gets its sigma +-2 deg certified too when --ko-proof is set (3 certificates instead of 1); off by default in v1, and the node records koProof: false so the caveat travels with the claim.

8. SYMMETRIES = DEDUPE ONLY (from RFL). resolvePush (index.html:2968) is Gauss-Seidel in a fixed (i,j) loop order, so a reflected or relabelled multi-contact push is not bit-identical. The x/y reflections (side arcs at (+-66.667, 0) with spans symmetric about the x axis: exact for geometry), the 180-degree rotation and the tripod's 120-degree relabelling are used only to build a dedupe key for seeds and lookups. No certificate is transported. TFG's canonicalisation is cut down to that.

9. REPLY SIGNATURE RULE CORRECTED (judge 1's objection to TFG). A reply family is one sweep, so its pairs set grows monotonically in t1; demanding equal replySig between consecutive stops would refuse nearly every pushing arm. Continuity of the landing along t1 is by construction (one integration). Signature consistency is demanded where different sweeps are compared: the witness sweep's moveSig (pairs, onset within 5 deg) between the two ends of a gap, and the child's dead profile (per arm: the certifying attacker arc sequence).

10. MEMO AT ITS MEASURED VALUE. swingLimitMemo removes the attacker's repeated limit calls along contact-free replies; measured hit rate 44% at these pushing positions, so verifyAllReplies drops from 44-70 s to ~30-40 s, not to 20 s. All budgets below use the measured numbers (load 5-6 today).

11. SO(2) FIBRE EXTENSION (RFL) deferred to v2: the physics is exactly rotation-invariant about the board centre (swing() uses only rotateAround, segment closest points and hypot to the centre), so any certified point, arc or ball could be extended along the board azimuth for the price of limit sweeps only. Not in v1 because v1's regions are thin and the frontier is level 3, not coverage.

JBT's closed-form interval containment (coveredBy, validated in scratchpad/contain-test.js) is kept in reserve for lookups once arcs and balls have radii; v1's lookup is the L1 ball / arc-tube test below.

## Representation

FILE: nn/family-graph.jsonl, one JSON row per node, written with atomic appends; nn/forced-win.js gains a loader (loadGraph) and the functions below. Poses are the game's own numbers: pieces = [{x,y,rot} blue, {x,y,rot} red], rotations raw as in dead-positions.jsonl.

METRIC: d(p,q) = sum over the two pieces of (hypot(dx,dy) + R*|drot|), R = 23.095u. Rotation is compared raw (no 120-degree folding in the metric). This is the only metric in the file; every 'u' below is in it.

NODE = { id, plies, side, kind, pose[6], eps, margin, lip, need, reach, star, arc, child, samples, engine, koProof, seed, key }
- plies: integer >= 2. side = who is to move at every position of the node. Even plies: side LOSES within plies plies (plies 2 = dead point, 4 = dead-in-3). Odd plies: side WINS (3 = win-in-2, 5 = win-in-3). Level = ceil(plies/2). Level = graph distance from the throw.
- kind 'point' (even plies): region = { q : d(q, pose) <= eps }, an L1 ball in the joint 6-D space; eps = 0 until certifyStar has run. margin = W = worst dead margin (u past the rim) at pose from deadCertificate (plies 2) or min over samples of (child W - need) (plies >= 4). lip, need as in the existing certificates. reach = the six per-arm envelopes {lo, hi, sig} of the victim's box that the star was certified over (null for a bare point).
- kind 'arc' (odd plies): arc = { pv, dir, span:[s0,s1] rad, step, perSample:[{s, minGap, lim, limSig}] }, child = id of the plies-1 point it lands on. Position at s: pose with pose[side] replaced by moverAt(pose[side], pv, -dir, s) (the child's attacker unwound backwards along its own foot pv). Every arc position plays (pv, dir) to stop s and lands EXACTLY on child (moverAt(moverAt(p,pv,-dir,s),pv,dir,s) = p identically). eps = the arc's tube radius (L1, both pieces): region = union over s in span of the L1 ball of radius eps around arcPose(s). eps = min(child.eps/3, minGap - MIND - 0.17u, limSlack) per graft 6 (0 until the child is starred).
- samples (even plies >= 4, the constructive evidence): per victim arm (pv, dir): [{ t1, landing[6], choice:{pvA, dirA, sigma, moveSig:{pairs,onset}}, target: nodeId | 'THROW', W, gapU, lip, need }] -- at every certified victim stop, the attacker's certified answer and the plies-2 child it lands on. This IS the policy simCheckLost plays.
- star: null | { h, probes:[12 x {axis, sign, status, W, profileOK}], lipStar } from certifyStar; eps = min(h, W_centre/lipStar).
- engine: { trials, agree } from the engine check of the node's own claim (simCheckDead over the ball for points; forward play into the child for arcs; simCheckLost for plies >= 4).
- key: the dedupe key = the pose canonicalised under the D2 reflections, the 180-degree rotation and the 120-degree relabellings, rounded to 0.1u / 0.1u/R; used to skip duplicate seeds, never to transport a certificate.

MEMBERSHIP: lookup(q, plies, side, graph) -> { node, depth } or null. Points: depth = eps - d(q, pose). Arcs: depth = eps - min over the arc's perSample s of d(q, arcPose(s)) (chord error <= R*step^2/8, 3e-5u at a 1-degree step, added to need). No canonicalisation on lookup in v1 (the graph is a tree of real positions; a hit is a genuine coincidence, not a symmetry).

LEVEL 1 IS NEVER STORED: 'attacker to move, throws now' is decided by six sweeps (bestThrow, existing), and 'thrown for a box of victim poses' by certifyThrowBox (existing). Both are membership procedures, not nodes.

WHAT MAKES THE JOINT DEPENDENCE EXPLICIT: an arc node is a one-parameter family whose victim pose is fixed and whose attacker pose is generated by a move; a point node is a ball around both poses; a plies-4 point does not test containment of a victim family in a pre-existing thin region at all -- it constructs the plies-2 child ON each landing (both poses as they actually are after the reply) and certifies the chain W(t1) > need along t1 with the child's own gap length (graft 4). Regions are thin by honesty, not by design: they are exactly as fat as the measured margins allow.

## Levels 1-3: what is certified and by what test

LEVEL 1 (plies 1; attacker `side` to move throws now). Certified by: bestThrow(pieces, side, REPLICA).margin > 0 for a point; certifyThrowBox for a box of the victim's poses (existing: grid + Lipschitz x3 floor 1 + pairs/onset consistency). Falsified by simCheck (engine plays the sweep on random poses in the box). Unchanged; used as the membership test at every leaf of levels 2 and 3.

LEVEL 2, two node kinds.
(a) LOST(2) point + ball: 'victim `side` to move; every legal move (six arms, every stop up to the arm's REACH ENVELOPE) is answered by a throw', for every joint pose q with d(q, pose) <= eps. Certified by: deadCertificate at the centre and at the 12 star probes (+-h on each of the six axes, rotations +-h/R), each = verifyAllReplies with the reply families swept to reachEnvelope(victim box of half-width h).hi (envelope refused on a stopping-event mismatch between grid neighbours, hi includes one substep), verdict dead at all 13, profiles consistent (same certifying attacker-arc sequence per arm, segment boundaries moved by at most one gap); lipStar = max(1, 3 * max over axes |W(+h) - W(-h)| / (2h)); eps = min(h, W_centre / lipStar). Reply that throws the attacker: the family's maxFootR is monotone in the stop, so one sweep to hi refuses it for every stop (existing). Falsified by: simCheckDeadBall(n = 25 poses uniform in the L1 ball x 8 random legal engine moves each; the engine's own limits, then the engine sweeps the six attacker arcs) -- every trial must end in a throw. A contradiction halts the run as a bug.
(b) WIN(3) arc + tube: 'attacker `side` to move from any position within eps of arcPose(s), s in span, wins by playing (pv, dir) to stop s: the swing is legal, touches nothing, lands inside the child's ball'. Certified by: for each s on the 1-degree ladder from 2 to 359 deg (uncapped): unwound pose on the board (hub <= edgeU - R - edgeEps, no foot off); law sweep of the forward move with the new minGap tracking; the engine plays the forward swing (load, pinFoot, applySwing in 1-degree steps) and both pieces must land on the child within 0.05u / 1e-3 rad and !atLimit (existing --back-from loop); limSig = (limitReason, justCrossed ids, nearest-line foot) from limitAt at the unwound pose; arc segments split where limSig changes or minGap <= MIND + 0.17u; eps = min(child.eps / 3, minGap - MIND - 0.17u, limSlack) with limSlack from 12 limitAt probes at +-eps on the attacker's axes requiring lim >= s + 0.4 deg with the same limSig (the rigid-map factor 3 is a proof, graft 6; limSlack is heuristic-class, same as everywhere else). The ko interval is the span itself (>= 2 deg). Falsified by: simCheckArc(n = 30): random s in span and random q in the tube; the engine plays the forward swing; the landing must be inside the child's ball and !atLimit; then simCheckDead(landing, 8 moves).

LEVEL 3, two node kinds.
(a) LOST(4) point: 'victim `side` to move at pose; every legal move (six arms, every stop to the envelope) lands in a position where the attacker either throws now or has a certified move into a LOST(2) node'. Certified by certifyLostPoint: per arm, fam = replyFamily swept to reach.hi (reach = reachEnvelope of the point's own tiny box, half-width 0.05u, so the envelope also absorbs the substep phase); refuse if fam.out.maxFootR > EDGE; stops every 2 deg as prefixes of the record; at each stop the landing L(t1) = [moverAt(victim), record(t1)]; findWin(L): (i) bestThrow(L).margin > 0 -> target THROW with the throw arc's margin as W and its (pairs, onset) as moveSig; (ii) else lookup(L, 2, attacker) with depth > 0 -> edge, W = depth; (iii) else extendDead(L, hint): one recorded sweep along the hinted attacker arm gives every sigma at once (throw at some sigma -> THROW); deadScreen (verifyAllReplies with screen: 8-degree stops, no gap certification, ~6 s) at sigma-2, sigma, sigma+2 deg; deadCertificate on the best -> a new LOST(2) node D(t1) with W and an edge; else widen (arm at 6 deg screened, then the other five arms); nothing -> certifyForcedIn2(L, attacker): every candidate refuted by an engine-confirmed escape -> ESCAPE-in-3 at (pv, dir, t1) (a real position, checkable), else UNRESOLVED. Gaps [A,B] along t1: same (pvA, dirA) at both ends, moveSig consistent (pairs equal, onset within 5 deg), child profiles consistent, gapU = 2R*dt1 + 3*attPath[A,B] + 2R*|dsigma| (graft 4), lip = max(1, 3|W_A - W_B|/gapU), need = lip*gapU/2, W_A > need and W_B > need; failing gaps get a new stop at the midpoint (back to findWin) down to the engine's substep; at the substep still failing -> UNRESOLVED (no sliver acceptance above the throw level). Verdict DEAD-IN-3 iff every gap of every legal arm is certified; margin = min over samples of (W - need); eps = 0 until certifyStar(node, h) is run at plies 4 (each probe re-running certifyLostPoint, so lazily, only where a lookup needs it). Falsified by: simCheckLost(n = 30): the engine plays a random legal victim move (its own limits, random stop), then the stored policy (the nearest sample's choice with sigma interpolated between the two neighbouring samples), then the six attacker sweeps: every trial must end in a throw of the victim within two attacker moves; any trial that does not is a bug.
(b) WIN(5) arc: unwindArcs applied to a LOST(4) point exactly as in level 2(b), child = that point; eps = 0 while the child's eps is 0 (tube radius requires the child's star), the arc itself still a certified curve: every position on it wins in three by the engine-played forward swing plus the child's certificate.

The evidential status at every level is the codebase's: exact where the map is rigid (victim landing, arc landing, contact-free gap bounds, the 3x rigid amplification, the ko interval), finite-difference x3 floor 1 elsewhere (throw margins along reply arms, child W along t1, star slopes, pushed landings, limit envelopes), grazing guarded by signatures (witness sweep pairs/onset, child profiles, limit stopping events), and every certificate followed by the engine playing random positions of the claim.

// -- contact-law.js: one addition --
function minGapOf(a, b, N = 12) -> number
  // pure geometry, the closest-approach distance of two tripods (the leg-leg / hub-leg / hub-hub terms of `touching`, minimum instead of boolean; no chord pre-filter)
  arcs A, B = arcsOf(a,N), arcsOf(b,N); best = Infinity
  for i,j: best = min(best, arcClosest(A[i], B[j]).dist)                     // leg-leg vs MIND
  for j: best = min(best, pointArcClosest(hubA, B[j]).dist - (HUBLEGD - MIND)) // hub-leg, shifted so every term compares to MIND
  for i: same for hubB vs A[i]; best = min(best, hypot(hub diff) - (2*HUBR - MIND))
  return best            // contact-free iff best >= MIND; export it and export MIND
swing(): add K.gap -> out.minGap = min over steps of minGapOf(active, opp) (one line at the end of each step).

// -- limits --
const limitMemo = new Map();
function swingLimitMemo(pieces, active, pv, dir) -> lim
  key = [x,y,rot of pieces[active] rounded 1e-7, pv, dir].join(',')   // the limit is a function of the mover's pose alone (pinFoot + crossingSubstep read only the active piece)
  if memo has key return it; lim = limitAt(pieces, active, pv, dir).lim; memo.set(key, lim); return lim
  // replyFamily and throwMargin call this instead of swingLimit; measured hit rate 44% at the dead points
function limitAt(pieces, active, pv, dir) -> { lim, reason, crossed, foot, sig }
  g = load(pieces, active); eng.pinFoot(pv); guard = 0
  while (!g.atLimit && |g.netRad| < 2*pi && guard++ < 1200) eng.applySwing(dir * 1 deg)   // NOT simMoveToLimit: its 170-degree cap is the AI's (index.html:3402), applySwing has none
  feet = g.pieces[active].feet(); foot = argmin over i != pv of eng.lineDistOf(feet[i], id) for id in eng.nearLineIds(feet[i], CFG.touchEps + 0.2)
  return { lim: |g.netRad|, reason: g.limitReason, crossed: unique sorted g.justCrossed, foot, sig: `${reason}|${crossed}|${foot}` }
function reachEnvelope(pieces, mover, pv, dir, box, n = 5) -> { lo, hi, sig, refused }
  grid n^3 over box (the MOVER's x, y, rot only; the other piece is irrelevant to the limit): L[i,j,k] = limitAt(...)
  refused = any two grid neighbours with different sig -> `arm (pv,dir): stopping event changes between cells (i,j,k)-(i',j',k'): sigA vs sigB`
  slope = max over neighbour pairs |dL| / (cell L1 length); lip = max(1, 3*slope); allow = lip * (half cell diagonal in L1) + 0.4 deg   // one substep: the ladder phase depends on the caller's dA
  return { lo: min L - allow, hi: max L + allow, sig: L[centre].sig, refused }
  // D-claims (victim's replies) use hi; W-claims (attacker's witness legality) use lo

// -- level-2 certificates --
function deadCertificate(pieces, victim, K, opts = { reach: null, screen: false }) -> verdict (verifyAllReplies' shape + profile)
  = verifyAllReplies with three changes: (1) replyFamily(..., limOverride = reach ? reach[arm].hi : swingLimitMemo(...)); stops past the sample's own limit are still certified (superset of replies);
    (2) opts.screen: stops every 8 deg, no gap loop, returns { minBest } in ~6 s; (3) returns profile: per arm the sequence of certifying attacker arcs with their pairs, as segments over t2
function certifyStar(node, h, K) -> node (eps, star, reach set) 
  box = victim's pose +- h (rot +- h/R); node.reach = reachEnvelope for the six arms; if any refused -> node.star = { refused } and return
  probes = centre and +-h on each of the 6 axes (12); each = deadCertificate(probePose, victim, K, { reach })   // plies 2; at plies >= 4 the same with certifyLostPoint
  refuse unless all 13 dead and profilesConsistent(centre.profile, probe.profile) (same arc sequence per arm, boundaries within one gap)
  lipStar = max(1, 3 * max over axes |W(+h) - W(-h)| / (2h)); node.eps = min(h, W_centre / lipStar); node.margin = W_centre
  node.engine = simCheckDeadBall(node, 25, 8)
function simCheckDeadBall(node, nPoses, nMoves) -> { agree, n, fails }
  for t < nPoses: q = pose + a uniform random L1 vector of length <= eps spread over the 6 axes (rot axes divided by R); s = simCheckDead(q, side, nMoves) (existing: engine limits, random stop, engine sweeps); count s.agree === s.n

// -- level-2 -> level-3 arcs --
function unwindArcs(node, K) -> Arc[]   (appends WIN(plies+1) nodes; child = node.id; att = 1 - node.side)
  for pv, dir: for deg = 2..359 step 1: prev = pose with prev[att] = moverAt(pose[att], pv, -dir, s)
    ok = onBoard(prev[att]) && enginePlaysForward(prev, att, pv, dir, s) lands on pose within 0.05u/1e-3 rad for BOTH pieces && !atLimit   // the existing --back-from loop
    out = swing(prev, att, pv, dir, s, { ...K, gap: true, trace: true }); ok &= out.trace.length === 0 && out.minGap > MIND + 0.17
    ls = limitAt(prev, att, pv, dir); ok &= ls.lim >= s + 0.4 deg; record { s, minGap: out.minGap, lim: ls.lim, limSig: ls.sig }
    split the s-ladder into segments on ok and on limSig changes; each segment with >= 2 samples becomes an arc node
  arc.eps = min(child.eps / 3, min over samples (minGap - MIND - 0.17), limSlack(arc))   // graft 6; limSlack = 12 limitAt probes at +-eps on the attacker's axes at the arc's two ends: lim >= s + 0.4 deg with the same limSig, else halve eps and retry (3 rounds), else eps = 0
  arc.engine = simCheckArc(arc, 30)
function simCheckArc(arc, n) -> { agree, n, fails }

## Open questions

1. PHASE_TOL is unmeasured and load-bearing. applySwing splits dA into ceil(|dA|/0.4deg) EQUAL substeps, so an engine turn of arbitrary phase integrates a different ladder from the law's fixed 0.4-degree record; today's measurement shows the LIMIT alone moves 0.1-0.3 deg with the observation step. If the pushed-pose p100 comes out above ~0.5u, level-3 gaps on pushing arms will not certify at 2-degree stops and the record must be re-swept at the gap's own phase (a swing() variant that takes the engine's substep size) -- cheap, but it changes the family from one sweep per arm to one per gap.

2. Do the four candidate rows (the loser two moves before each throw) certify dead-in-3 at all? level3-shot1 found, at a different position, one arm with an escape after every attacker move was refuted; the screen (~20 min per seed) decides that before any hours are spent. If all four escape, the level-3 layer is complete but empty for these games, and seeds must come from unwindTwice (a WIN(3) arc position with the victim's played arm unwound contact-free) or from the danger maps' ply-3 cells.

3. Two of the four dead points (0.845u and 1.004u worst margin) may refuse the star at h = 0.5 (need = h*lipStar >= 0.5u leaves no margin); h = 0.25 halves the ball. Regions at ~1u margins are ~0.25u balls, and level-3 children (W ~1.25u measured at the one landing that has been tried) will be the same. Reuse via lookup will rarely fire; the graph is a tree of certified lines with thin tubes. Fat regions need fat margins, which means deeper throws (victim feet further out) -- a seed-selection question, not a representation one.

4. Reach envelopes can refuse on tangential line crossings: the crossing-budget limit jumps where a foot's arc grazes a printed line's 0.81u band or a corner-merge branch flips. Refusals name the axis and the cells; the fallback is a 9x9x9 grid and a split along the named axis, but a box that straddles a tangency is genuinely two regions and must be split, not averaged.

5. The pushed-landing amplification is measured, not derived, when the attacker's witness pushes the victim (3-4 of 6 arms at these points push): the child's victim pose then depends on sigma through the law, and the 3x rigid constant does not apply. certifyLostPoint uses the finite difference of the child position between the two gap ends x3 with the witness moveSig consistent -- the existing standard -- but it is the thinnest estimate in the file. A per-gap check that the witness sweep's minGap and contact onset move continuously between A and B would tighten it.

6. Ko is certified only on demand (--ko-proof, 3x the child certificates). Without it a LOST(4) node's policy can be voided in a game where the victim's reply was contact-free, the witness is contact-free, and the child position was on the board earlier -- rare, and the node says koProof: false, but a claim about positions is then a claim about most histories, not all.

7. simMoveToLimit's 170-degree cap is inside every EXISTING certificate (verifyAllReplies, certifyDeadBox, the 29 throw boxes' swingLimit, the 4 dead points). None of the measured reply limits at the four points exceeds 111 degrees, so those certificates are unaffected in fact, but the switch to limitAt should be followed by a re-run of --mine on the four games so nn/dead-positions.jsonl carries the uncapped verdicts.

8. Level 4 by this route costs ~240 LOST(4) certificates per LOST(6) point (each 3-6 core-hours): out of reach on this machine. The two ideas that could change that are the SO(2) fibre (RFL: the physics is exactly rotation-invariant, so every node extends along the board azimuth for limit sweeps only -- the one fat dimension available) and JBT's interval containment on balls with radii, so that lookups hit whole gaps instead of points. Both are v2 and both need the v1 nodes to exist first.

9. Symmetry transport is left off because resolvePush's Gauss-Seidel order breaks bit-exactness under relabelling; if a measurement shows relabelled/reflected pushes agree to < 0.01u over 200 events, the graph could be folded 12x (D2 x 180-degree x tripod), but that is a measurement to make, not an assumption.

10. Machine: all budgets above are at today's load 5-6 (13 dead certificates ~9 min, an envelope ~1.3 min per box, a LOST(4) candidate 3-6 core-hours); the trainer and two brain-map processes hold ~2 cores. Run the four seeds as separate node processes appending to nn/family-graph.jsonl through the atomic writer; a lookup that hits a node written by another process is fine because nodes are immutable once appended.

## The three designs, as judged

### Joint boxes unwound into tubes (JBT): star-certified 6-D dead boxes, exact-kinematics win tubes, interval containment

**Representation.** Everything lives in nn/joint-boxes.js (requires contact-law.js and forced-win.js) and is persisted one row per object in nn/joint-atlas.jsonl. Poses are the game's own numbers: a piece is {x, y, rot}, index 0 = blue, 1 = red, rotations stored raw (as in dead-positions.jsonl), and the metric everywhere is d(q, q') = hypot(dx, dy) + R·|drot| with R = 23.095.

1. DEAD BOX (level k, the side `mover` to move loses within k):
   JointBox = { id, kind: 'dead', level: k, mover: 0|1,
     box: [ {x:[lo,hi], y:[lo,hi], rot:[lo,hi]}, {x:[..], y:[..], rot:[..]} ]   // indexed by piece, so box[mover] is the victim's 3-D box and box[1-mover] the attacker's; each rot interval is narrower than 2*pi/3
     cert: { design: 'star', h: [hm, ho],            // half-widths of the mover's box (x, y in u; rot = hm/R) and of the other's box
             dmax,                                     // sqrt(2*hm^2 + 3*ho^2): farthest box point from its nearest star sample (drop one mover axis via the face sample), for hm >= ho
             minMargin, lip, need,                     // as certifyDeadBox: need = lip*dmax, lip = max(1, 3*steepest star finite difference)
             envelope: { '0,1': [lo, hi], ... },       // per victim arm, the certified interval containing every legal stop for every pose in the box (rad)
             signatures: { '0,1': {pairs, onsetLo, onsetHi}, ... },
             engine: { n, agree } },                   // simCheckDeadBox
     seed: { p: [6 numbers], source } }
   The 13 star samples are the centre and centre +- h on each of the six axes (rot axes offset by h/R). A dead box at level 2 is certified by verifyAllReplies at the 13 samples (the existing certificate, swept to the envelope); at level >= 3 by containment of landing boxes in level-(k-1) tubes (below).

2. TUBE (level k+1 win region, the side `att` to move wins within k+1) = a dead box unwound along one attacker arm:
   Tube = { id, kind: 'win', level: k+1, att, base: <dead box id with mover = 1-att>, arm: [apv, adir], s: [s0, s1],   // rad, s0 = MIN_MOVE + 0.1 deg
            cert: { minGap, gapNeed, limMin, limNeed, engine: {n, agree} } }
   Its point set is T = { (qV, moverAt(qA, apv, -adir, sigma)) : (qV, qA) in base, sigma in [s0, s1] }: the victim box unchanged, the attacker box swept backwards along the arc of its own foot apv. This is "box x arc parameter": 6 box coordinates plus sigma; the attacker part is a union of rotated copies of base[att], long along the arc (28-139 deg at the four dead points), thin (2*ho) across it. The tube certifies: for every point of T the forward move (apv, adir, sigma) is legal, touches nothing, and therefore lands EXACTLY in base.

3. LANDING BOX (transient; stored only in demand records): LB = { att, arm, sInterval: [sa, sb], V: {x,y,rot intervals}, A: {x,y,rot intervals} } = an interval over-approximation of every landing point (att to move) of a victim reply (arm, stop in [sa, sb]) from every pose of a dead-box candidate. LB.V is exact interval arithmetic on moverAt (rigid rotation about a foot); LB.A is the hull of the 13 recorded pushed poses inflated by the Lipschitz allowance, or exactly the attacker's box while the reply is certified contact-free.

4. CONTAINMENT of an LB (or a point) in a tube, coveredBy(LB, tube, scanDeg = 0.25) -- exact, closed form, validated against brute force (0 violations in 40,000 random image points; 266/266 covered cases agree; the hull is tight):
   for sigma on the 0.25-degree ladder of [s0, s1] restricted to { sigma : LB.A.rot + adir*sigma inside base[att].rot } (an interval):
     a = 2R sin(adir*sigma/2), psi-interval = LB.A.rot + apv*2pi/3 + adir*sigma/2,
     hub' = hub + a*(sin psi, -cos psi)   [this is moverAt exactly: hub' = hub - (Rot(adir sigma) - I) R u(rot + apv 2pi/3)],
     X' = LB.A.x + range(a*sin psi over the psi-interval), Y' = LB.A.y + range(a*(-cos psi)) (interval ends plus interior critical points pi/2 + k pi resp. k pi),
     covered at this sigma iff X' inside base[att].x, Y' inside base[att].y, LB.V inside base[1-att] (plain interval inclusion).
   The first passing sigma is returned, together with the whole passing sigma-interval (the attacker may stop anywhere in it -- this is what makes the witness ko-proof). A point query is the degenerate LB. Rotation is canonicalised first: the query's rot is shifted by k*2pi/3 to land in the box's rot interval, and the tube's foot index is relabelled apv_phys = (apv + k) mod 3 (foot i of the shifted labelling is physical foot i+k); unit-tested by checking the feet coincide. The union-of-tubes test is "some single tube contains the whole LB"; if none does, the s-interval is bisected down to the engine's substep (0.4 deg) before the interval is declared uncovered. Index: tubes grouped by att and by the 5u cell of the base victim box's centre; a query first rejects by LB.V vs base[1-att] and by |hub' - centre| > 2R + ho, then runs the sigma scan (microseconds per tube).

5. ATLAS = { boxes: JointBox[], tubes: Tube[] } loaded from nn/joint-atlas.jsonl; demand records ({ seedId, LB, status: 'uncovered'|'unresolved'|'refuted', witnessHint }) are appended to nn/joint-demand.jsonl.

**Algorithm.** Level k+1 from level k, all functions in nn/joint-boxes.js, REPLICA settings throughout (the engine's own knobs, 0.000u from the engine on 200 events):

STEP 0, base (k = 2), certifyDeadBoxJoint(centre, mover, hm, ho, K, oracle = 'throw'):
  a. starSamples(centre, hm, ho) -> 13 joint poses, dmax = sqrt(2hm^2 + 3ho^2).
  b. limitEnvelope(box[mover], mover, pv, dir) for each of the 6 arms: the swing limit is a property of the mover's pose alone (swingLimit), so it is gridded on the mover's 3-D box only: a 5x5x5 grid, 125 calls of load + eng.simMoveToLimit (records lim, G.limitReason, and the stopping event's foot and line id), 4-5 s per arm. upper = max lim + 3*slope*cellHalfDiag, lower likewise; refuse if neighbours disagree on the stopping event with a lim jump above the allowance (a tangential line crossing = the crossing rule's grazing surface). If upper < MIN_MOVE the arm is illegal over the whole box.
  c. verifyAllRepliesTo(sample, mover, K, envelopes): verifyAllReplies (unchanged logic: reply family as ONE law sweep with record, stops as prefixes, six throw margins per stop, Lipschitz-certified gaps with subdivision, signature consistency, verdicts dead/escape/unresolved) with replyFamily swept to envelope.upper instead of swingLimit, so stops that only a neighbouring pose can reach are certified too. All 13 samples must be dead.
  d. certificate: minMargin > need = lip*dmax, lip = max(1, 3*max over the 12 star differences |m_face - m_centre|/h). Reuses: load, swingLimit, throwMargin, signature, verifyAllReplies (refactored to accept a per-arm limit), simCheckDead.
  e. simCheckDeadBox(box, n = 25 poses x 8 engine moves) -- the existing --dead-box check, over the joint box.

STEP 1, unwindTubes(deadBox, K) -> tubes (level k+1, att = 1 - mover): for each attacker arm (apv, adir), climb s on a 1-degree ladder from s0 = MIN_MOVE + 0.1 deg to 170 deg (AI_SAFETY_CAP) and keep the largest s such that at all 13 samples (qV, qA):
  (i) contact-free backwards: minGap(qV, moverAt(qA, apv, -adir, s')) - MIND > gapNeed for every s' in [0, s] on a 0.5-degree ladder, where minGap is a new pure function (the leg-leg / hub-leg / hub-hub closest distances of contact-law's `touching`, returning the minimum, N = 12 segments = the engine's polyline) and gapNeed = 1*dmax + (R*sqrt(3))*(0.25 deg in rad) = dmax + 0.17u. This bound is rigorous: the gap is 1-Lipschitz per piece in the R-scaled metric (no point of a tripod is farther than R from its hub, so a pose change of d moves every point by at most d) and (R*sqrt(3) = 40u/rad)-Lipschitz in s' (every point of the swinging piece is within R*sqrt(3) of the pivot foot: the legs project onto the radial hub-foot segments).
  (ii) legal forward: from the unwound start moverAt(qA, apv, -adir, s), load + eng.simMoveToLimit(apv, adir) must reach |netRad| >= s + limNeed with atLimit not yet set, limNeed = 3*(star slope of that forward limit)*dmax; the unwound start must also be on the board with margin dmax (feet radius is 1-Lipschitz). Suffix monotonicity (a shorter forward swing from a later start along the same arc is at least as legal, because whatever a foot starts on is the turn's free opening episode) is relied on and checked by the engine in (iv).
  (iii) emit the tube if s1 - s0 >= 1 deg, cert = {minGap, gapNeed, limMin, limNeed}.
  (iv) simCheckTube(tube, n = 30): random (qV, qA) in base, random sigma in [s0, s1]; the engine plays the forward swing from the unwound pose; both pieces must land inside base (victim untouched) with atLimit false; then simCheckDead at the landing (8 moves). Reuses moverAt, load, eng.pinFoot/applySwing exactly as --back-from does.

STEP 2, certifyDeadBoxJoint(centre, mover, hm, ho, K, oracle = tubes) -- the level-(k+1) dead box:
  a-b. as in STEP 0 (13 star samples; limit envelopes on the mover's 3-D box).
  c. per sample per arm: fam = replyFamily swept to envelope.upper with {record: true, trace: true}; refuse the box if fam.maxFootR + lipFoot*dmax >= EDGE at any sample (the reply must not throw the attacker; the furthest foot is monotone in the stop, lipFoot = max(1, 3*slope)).
  d. per arm, contact bookkeeping across the 13 samples: s_c = (min onset over samples) - 5 deg is the certified contact-free prefix end, tightened rigorously by the same minGap Lipschitz check as STEP 1(i) applied to the victim's own arc (mover's points within R*sqrt(3) of its pivot); beyond s_c the leg-pair signatures restricted to the interval and the onsets (within 5 deg) must agree between all samples, else that s-interval is refused ("graze").
  e. per arm, walk s-intervals of 2 deg from MIN_MOVE to envelope.upper, bisecting on failure down to 0.4 deg: build LB:
       LB.V = exact image intervals of box[mover] under moverAt over [sa, sb] (the containment formula with the roles swapped: victim hub' = hub + a*(sin psi, -cos psi) with psi ranging over rot-interval + pv*2pi/3 + dir*[sa,sb]/2, a over 2R sin(dir*[sa,sb]/2); rot' = rot + dir*[sa,sb]);
       LB.A = box[1-mover] exactly for s <= s_c; otherwise the hull of the 13 samples' recorded pushed poses over [sa, sb], inflated in x, y and R*rot by (1 + 3*pushSlope)*dmax + PHASE_TOL, pushSlope = max over samples and stops of (|delta pushed pose| - own-axis displacement)/h (measured at the two probed points: total slopes 1.0-1.8 per u, i.e. push part 0-0.8).
       coveredBy(LB, tube) over the tubes with tube.att = 1 - mover and tube.level = k (and any lower level; a level-1 throw box is a tube with s = [0,0] and the throw arm as witness, so the 29 certified throw boxes plug in as level-1 tubes once re-certified jointly by certifyThrowBoxJoint = certifyThrowBox on the 13-sample star). The covering sigma-interval must be at least koDeg = 2 deg wide.
  f. verdict: dead-(k+1) if every interval of every arm whose envelope reaches MIN_MOVE is covered; else 'uncovered' with the list of demand records (arm, s-interval, LB, the centre landing point). Certificate need is implicit: LB already contains every landing of every box pose. simCheckDeadN(box, n): random pose in the box, random legal victim move played by the engine, look up the covering tube for the engine's landing point, play its sigma in the engine, then simCheckDead at the result (level 2 base) -- any failure is a bug in this file, as with simCheck at level 1.

STEP 3, growAtlas(demand, k): the atlas is demand-driven. For each uncovered LB (centre point P', att to move): witnessSearch(P', att, k): the engine's depth-2 plan (nnPlanFor, as --win-map does) as the first candidate, then the candidate ladder of certifyForcedIn2 (6 arms x 3-degree stops); for each candidate the after-position gets the level-k dead test (verifyAllReplies at k = 2; the tube test at k >= 3). A dead point found -> certifyDeadBoxJoint around it at level k with (hm, ho) chosen so LB fits: hm >= LB.V half-width + 0.25u, ho >= LB.A half-width + 0.1u, shrinking (halving) until the margin certificate passes -> unwindTubes -> re-run STEP 2 for the seed. An escape at every candidate (engine-confirmed by simCheckEscape at k = 2) -> the seed is refuted at that reply (a real escape from the would-be dead-(k+1) box). Dead boxes are chained along each victim arm at pitch 2*(hm - LB.V half-width) until the arm is covered.

STEP 4, next level: the certified dead-(k+1) boxes feed STEP 1 (unwindTubes) to give level-(k+2) tubes; the same STEP 2 with oracle = those tubes gives dead-(k+2) boxes. The CLI: node nn/joint-boxes.js --dead-box <6 numbers> --mover m --hm 0.5 --ho 0.3 [--level 2|3] ; --unwind <box id> ; --grow <seed 6 numbers> --mover m --level 3 ; --check <box or tube id>.

**Soundness.** Induction on the level, relative to the engine and to the certificates already trusted at levels 1-2.

Tube (exact): a point P' = (qV', qA') is in tube T iff qV' is in base[1-att] and some sigma in [s0, s1] satisfies moverAt(qA', apv, adir, sigma) in base[att]. That is the verbatim definition of T, and coveredBy computes the image of a landing box under moverAt in closed form (hub' = hub + 2R sin(adir sigma/2)(sin psi, -cos psi), rot' = rot + adir sigma), an over-approximating interval that is tight and was checked against brute force. The forward swing (apv, adir, sigma) is legal at P': the tube certifies, at all 13 star samples of base, the engine's own limit from the unwound start beyond s1 with an allowance, and the unwound start on the board; the swing is contact-free: the gap along the whole arc back to base clears MIND by gapNeed = dmax + 0.17u, and the gap is a pure geometric function with PROVABLE Lipschitz constants (1 per piece in the R-scaled metric, 40u/rad in sigma), so the only assumption there is the sample ladder (0.5 deg) and the star's covering radius dmax -- not a finite-difference estimate. Contact-free means the pushed piece does not move (the law: the least motion keeping every gap >= 2rho is zero when no gap is violated; the engine's resolvePush does nothing without a contact), and the mover is kinematic (contact never stops it, header of forced-win.js), so the landing is exactly (qV', moverAt(qA', sigma)) in base, a certified level-k dead box: the attacker wins in k+1. The ko rule cannot void the witness because coveredBy returns a sigma-interval of at least koDeg, and koLegalizePlan nudges within it.

Dead box at level k+1: for every pose P in the box and every legal victim move (arm, s): (1) s lies in the arm's envelope -- the limit is a function of the mover's 3 coordinates, gridded 5x5x5 with the stopping event's identity checked between neighbours and a 3x-slope allowance; the envelope over-approximates the reachable stops, and over-approximation only adds obligations, so it is sound whenever the envelope is; (2) the landing lies in LB(arm, interval): the victim part is exact interval arithmetic on a rigid rotation (no estimate at all); the attacker part is exact (identity) on the contact-free prefix, whose end is certified by the same provable gap Lipschitz bound; on the contact suffix it is the law's recorded pushed pose at the 13 samples plus a Lipschitz allowance from the star's finite differences (safety 3, floor 1, applied to the push-induced part on top of the exact identity part), plus PHASE_TOL for the engine stopping between 0.4-degree substeps, with the same contact-history consistency rule as certifyThrowBox (same leg pairs, first touch within 5 deg between samples) so a grazing surface inside the box is refused rather than averaged over -- this is exactly the level-1 argument, applied to the pushed pose instead of the throw margin, and it inherits level 1's status (29 boxes, 1160 engine-confirmed poses, 0 contradictions) and its caveat (a graze can hide between samples; the engine check is the backstop); (3) LB is inside a tube, exact; (4) by the tube, the attacker wins in k. The base case is the existing certifyDeadBox argument on a 6-D star (worstMargin Lipschitz across the star, verifyAllReplies inside), with the sweep extended to the envelope so that no pose in the box has a legal stop the sample did not certify. The "reply throws the attacker" case is excluded by the monotone furthest-foot bound with the same allowance.

Where the heuristic (finite-difference Lipschitz x3, floor 1) enters, and only there: throw margins along reply arms (inside verifyAllReplies, existing), the level-2 worst margin across the star, the pushed landing's slope across the star, the swing-limit envelope, and the tube's forward-legality allowance. Where it does not enter (exact): victim landing intervals, tube containment, contact-free gap bounds, the landing of a contact-free swing, the ko interval. Where grazing is handled: contact-history signatures per s-interval (leg pairs, onset) for the pushed landing; stopping-event identity for the crossing rule; the contact-free prefix is certified rigorously so a graze at the START of contact cannot hide. Falsification is built in at every layer: simCheckDeadBox (level 2), simCheckTube (the engine plays the forward swing and must land inside base), simCheckDeadN (engine reply, engine witness from the tube, engine throws), and a contradiction is a bug in joint-boxes.js, never a rounding.

3. If refusals cluster, exclude the unphysical corner by requiring minGap >= MIND - 0.05u over the box (a rigorous geometric check) and splitting the box.

The engine stops a human or AI swing at arbitrary angles with a rescaled last substep, while the record is on a fixed 0.4-degree ladder; the header's "ideal + 0.4-degree step alone: max 0.75u" bounds step-size sensitivity, not phase. Measure it before trusting level >= 3: 200 recorded contact events, engine stopped at random angles vs the law's record interpolated, take the p100 of the pushed-pose difference as PHASE_TOL. ESC_TOL (0.75u) exists for the same reason on the escape side.

5. The push-slope safety factor is expensive: (1 + 3 x 0.8) x dmax3 inflates LB.A to 3.4 dmax3 on the worst arms, forcing ho = 0.5 at level 2. 6. Contact-free tubes only. A level-k witness move that pushes the victim is not representable as a tube (the landing victim pose depends on sigma), so W_k is under-approximated and dead-(k+1) seeds get refused as 'uncovered' when the only winning witness pushes -- and at these positions the attacker's arms mostly push (3-4 of 6 arms per point). ### Reduced-frame ledger cells (RFL): 5-D rotation-invariant cells with a cheap azimuth fibre for the crossing budget, explicit D-cells, implicit W-regions tested at the true pose

**Representation.** COORDINATES. For a joint pose P = (victim v=(vx,vy,vrot), attacker a=(ax,ay,arot)) define
  gamma = atan2(vy, vx)                       -- the victim's board azimuth (the fibre)
  rho   = hypot(vx, vy)                       -- victim hub radius (0..~66)
  psi   = vrot - gamma  (mod 2pi)             -- victim heading relative to its own radial line
  (u,w) = R(-vrot) * (a_hub - v_hub)          -- attacker hub in the victim's body frame
  delta = arot - vrot   (mod 2pi)             -- attacker heading in the victim's body frame
zeta = (rho, psi, u, w, delta) in 5-D; reduce(pieces, victim) -> {zeta, gamma}; lift(zeta, gamma, victim) -> pieces is its exact inverse (fp 1e-13). Metric on zeta, as in the existing code: Euclidean on (d rho, R d psi, du, dw, R d delta), R = 23.095. Rotating the whole board by any angle about the centre changes gamma only. NOT folded: psi and delta stay in [0,2pi) (no tripod-120-degree transport, see soundness); gamma IS folded to [0, pi) by the exact 180-degree board symmetry.

EXACTLY INVARIANT under gamma (the "core"): the pushed piece's trajectory rec(alpha) for any arm (pv,dir) of either piece (swing() uses only relative geometry: rotateAround about a foot, arc-arc gaps, hub gaps); alpha_off = swing().offAt (first swing angle at which a victim foot passes EDGE = 67.167u -- the rim is a circle about the centre); the whole margin curve maxFootR(alpha) - EDGE; the contact signature (pairs, onset); the mover's own rim cap alpha_selfoff (feet leaving the board); ring crossings (rings are centred). NOT invariant (only under the 180-degree rotation, exactly): alpha_cross = the crossing-budget limit, because the two side arcs sit at (+-66.667, 0); hence swingLimit = min(alpha_selfoff, alpha_cross) and arm legality (limit >= MIN_MOVE = 2 deg). Everything non-invariant is ONE scalar per (absolute pose of the mover, arm): the "ledger".

LEVEL-k D-CELL (victim to move, loses in k attacker throws), the only stored object, one JSON row in nn/regions/D<k>.jsonl:
  { id, level:k, victim:0|1,
    zeta: {rho:[lo,hi], psi:[lo,hi], u:[lo,hi], w:[lo,hi], delta:[lo,hi]},   -- a closed 5-D box, 1u wide (0.5u/R rad on psi, delta) at the base level, split dyadically
    gamma: [[lo,hi],...]  (rad, in [0,pi); the 180-degree image is implied),
    grid: {h, samples},
    reach: [6 x {lo,hi,sig}]                 -- the victim's per-arm legal-reach envelope over zeta-box x gamma (from certifyLimitBox), sig = limit signature
    cert: {minWitnessMargin, lip, need, worstLip, replySig},
    witnesses: [{sample, arm, s:[s0,s1], kind:'throw'|'pre', cell, attArm, sPrime, margin}]   -- the attacker's replayable answer per reply segment
    engine: {poses, movesEach, agree}, seed: {game,row|'search'} }
It certifies the 6-D solid S = { lift(zeta,gamma) : zeta in box, gamma in Gamma } and its 180-degree rotation: both poses vary, the victim's absolute pose over rho x psi x gamma and the relative pose over (u,w,delta). Containment of a concrete pose is: reduce it, find the cell by zeta (dyadic index) with the cell's allowance, and check gamma in Gamma.

LEVEL-k W-REGIONS are NOT stored. W_k(Q) (attacker to move at Q) is decided by the procedure witnessW(k, Q) at Q's TRUE board pose: either bestThrow(Q) has margin > 0 (k>=1, existing), or (k>=2) some attacker arm (pv,dir) swung contact-free to an angle s' lands in S(C) for a stored D_{k-1} cell C. Because a contact-free attacker move leaves the victim untouched, it preserves (rho, psi, gamma) exactly, so the store is indexed by (rho, psi, gamma) bins and the candidate cells for Q are found by a 3-D lookup; inside a candidate, delta' = delta + dir*s' must fall in C.delta and (u',w') = rigid image in C.(u,w) -- solved in closed form, no sweep -- and only survivors get one replica sweep (clearance) and one engine limit sweep (legality). So the joint dependence is explicit in the D-cells and the W-region is the exact pre-image of a 6-D solid under the attacker's rigid move map, evaluated where it is needed.

**Algorithm.** Base: D_0 = empty; witnessW(1, Q) = bestThrow(Q, REPLICA) at the true pose (existing: swingLimit + swing); a stop is in W_1 iff margin > 0. New code lives in nn/forced-win-regions.js and reuses swing/IDEAL/REPLICA/feetOf/anyOff/R/EDGE/eng (contact-law.js) and load/swingLimit/throwMargin/bestThrow/signature/verifyAllReplies/replyFamily/moverAt/certifyForcedIn2/simCheckDead/simCheckEscape (forced-win.js).

**Soundness.** Claim: every pose in S(C) of a level-(k+1) D-cell is lost for the victim against the engine, by induction on k, with the same evidential status as the existing certificates (grid + Lipschitz allowance + signature consistency, engine-falsified), except for the parts marked EXACT which are proofs.

1. The physics core is EXACTLY rotation-invariant. swing() is built from rotateAround about a foot, arc/segment closest points and hypot to the centre; a rotation of both pieces about the board centre commutes with every one of those operations, the step count ceil(rad/0.4deg) is identical, and the engine's own resolvePush is the same code path (replica = engine at 0.000u on 200 events). So the trajectory, alpha_off, the margin curve, the contact signature and the rim cap at lift(zeta, gamma) do not depend on gamma beyond fp rounding (1e-12u against margins of ~1u). Therefore certifying the physics at ONE gamma per zeta sample and claiming it over Gamma is sound. The 180-degree rotation additionally maps arc a0 onto a1 (centres (-66.667,0) <-> (66.667,0), spans -72.5..72.5 <-> 107.5..252.5) with rings and rim fixed and no foot relabelling, so the whole engine including the crossing rule is invariant: folding Gamma is exact. Tripod relabelling and mirroring are NOT used for transport because the contact solver is Gauss-Seidel in a fixed (i,j) loop order: relabelling feet permutes the order and can change a multi-contact push at the 1e-3u level; indexing only.

2. Everything non-invariant funnels through the crossing-budget limit, and it is handled by envelopes with the right monotonicity: a D-claim only needs a SUPERSET of the victim's legal replies (reach.hi: more stops than the victim really has can only add obligations; a stop past the true limit that shows no witness is reported as a demand, not as dead), and a W-claim only needs a SUBSET of the attacker's reach (limSlack against lo). The limit as a function of pose is piecewise continuous with jumps exactly where a foot's circular path becomes tangent to a printed line's 0.81u touch band or a corner -- the line rule's grazing surface. It is guarded the way contact grazing is guarded today: the limit signature (reason, line ids, foot) must be identical across all grid neighbours, and the finite-difference Lipschitz allowance x 3 (floor 1 rad/u... i.e. 1 in the R-metric) covers the interior. This is heuristic-class, same as the level-1 boxes, and it is a gap the existing certifyDeadBox silently has (it takes each sample's own limit); here it is explicit and falsifiable (simCheckDeadK plays random lifted poses in the cell through the engine's own limits). Also: limitAt uses the engine's applySwing loop to 358 degrees, not simMoveToLimit's 170-degree AI cap, so a human reply longer than 170 degrees is inside the envelope -- verifyAllReplies as it stands is not safe against such replies.

3. The W-witness for a landing is decided at the TRUE pose and its transport across a reply segment / a cell uses EXACT Lipschitz constants where the map is rigid: a contact-free attacker swing to s' is an isometry of the attacker composed with a rotation about its foot; in the victim frame the landing (u',w') = (u,w) + (Rot(dir s') - I)(hub - foot) has Jacobian I in (u,w) and norm 2R sin(s'/2) in delta, so in the R-metric the map is 3-Lipschitz (a proof, no sampling); the min tube gap between rigid bodies is sqrt 2-Lipschitz in the same metric (a leg point moves at most |dpos| + R|dtheta|), so marginClear > sqrt 2 x distance certifies that NO pose in the neighbourhood touches -- contact grazing in the attacker's arrival is thereby excluded EXACTLY, which is stronger than the trace-empty test --back-from uses. The landing being inside C's box by marginGeom > 3 x distance is exact too. What remains heuristic-class on the W side is only limSlack (item 2) and, when the victim's reply pushed the attacker, the pushed pose's dependence on the stop and on zeta (finite-difference lip x 3 with the reply's pairs/onset signature consistent -- the existing rule; the reply family is one sweep so consistency along s is by construction, and across zeta samples it is checked).

4. Induction: for P in S(C_{k+1}) and any legal reply (arm, s): s <= true limit <= reach.hi, so the stop is in a certified segment; the landing Q is within the allowance of a sampled landing whose witness has margin > lip x distance, so at Q the attacker's witness swing to s' (throw: margin > 0 by the existing certificate; pre: clearance > 0 exactly, legal because limSlack envelope >= 0, landing strictly inside S(C_k) exactly) puts the game in S(C_k) with the victim to move, which by hypothesis is lost in k. The pass case: a victim with no legal arm is treated as escape (refuse), as verifyAllReplies does. Ko is history-dependent and ignored: it can only remove victim replies (fine for D) and could forbid the attacker's exact s' -- the s' interval from C.delta is 1u/R = 2.5 deg wide, above koDeg = 2, so a legal neighbour always exists (flagged in risks).

5. Where the Lipschitz assumption enters at all: the throw margin's lip (measured median 0.4, p90 0.85, max ~9 per u), the pushed attacker's pose lip after a pushing reply, and the limit envelope's lip. Where grazing enters: the reply's contact signature across zeta samples, the throw arm's signature along a segment, the limit signature everywhere, and the attacker's arrival clearance (exact). Every certified cell is followed by the engine playing random lifted poses (simCheckDeadK), and a contradiction is a bug in this file, not a rounding.

**Risks.** 1. Adaptive cell sizes per axis (split only the axis with the steep finite difference) is the fix if that happens.
3. Coverage: W_{k} pre-images use contact-free attacker arrivals only (as --back-from does); pushing arrivals into a D-cell are excluded, so a genuinely dead-in-3 position whose only refutation pushes the victim into the dead region will be refused with demands that certifyForcedIn2 cannot fill. Extension within the same representation: explicit W cells for pushing arrivals certified by the sampled ODE certificate (signature + finite-difference lip), queried by the same witnessW.
4. Tripod relabelling and mirror symmetry are not used for transport because of the Gauss-Seidel loop order in multi-contact pushes; if a later measurement shows relabelled pushes agree to < 0.01u the store shrinks 9x, but that is a measurement to make, not an assumption.
5. Ko is ignored: sound for the victim's side; for the attacker the s' interval (>= 2.5 deg from a 1u delta range) exceeds koDeg = 2 deg, but the cell's certified witness margin must survive moving s' by 2 deg (2R sin(1 deg) = 0.8u of landing displacement) -- add that to the required marginGeom, or accept that ko can very rarely force a different witness.
6. Demand-driven growth can chase escapes forever (a stop with no witness at level k+1 spawns a certifyForcedIn2 search that itself finds only 'unresolved' candidates); the budget and the memo cache bound it, and every unmet demand is recorded as a concrete (arm, s) segment so the refusal is informative.
7. Engine-vs-replica drift: everything is certified on the REPLICA; the 0.000u agreement is on 200 events. Every cell's simCheckDeadK is the standing falsification; a single contradiction must halt the run (bug), not be tallied.
8. The 2-degree reply grid and bisection to the 0.4-degree substep are inherited; a witness that changes arm within a substep stays 'unresolved', which is the honest verdict, but many such gaps on the alive segments will make D_2 cells refuse in practice.

### Tubed family graph: point/arc nodes generated by moves, with component-wise (attacker, victim) tube radii and constructive containment edges

**Representation.** Metric on the joint pose space (both poses, 6 numbers): d(p,q) = max( |Δa_xy|₂, R·|Δa_rot|, |Δv_xy|₂, R·|Δv_rot| ), R = 23.095u, i.e. the existing convention "rotation distance = R·dθ", taken per piece. A region is NEVER a coordinate box in 6-D. It is a set of positions GENERATED by moves from a root pose (mover's pose analytic via moverAt, pushed piece's pose from the recorded sweep), thickened by a tube radius that is a PAIR eps = (eps_a, eps_v), one radius per piece. The pair is what makes the joint dependence explicit and cheap: the two landing maps have different Lipschitz constants on the two components (see soundness).

One node = one JSON line in nn/family-graph.jsonl:
  { id, plies, side, kind, pose[6], sym, eps:[eps_a, eps_v], margin, lip, need, star, samples, arc, child, engine }
- plies: integer >= 2 = plies to the throw from any position of the node. Even plies = LOST for `side` (side to move loses by force), odd = WIN for `side`. plies 2 = dead point, 3 = win-in-2, 4 = dead-in-3, 5 = win-in-3. (Their "level" = ceil(plies/2).) Level = graph distance.
- side: who is to move at every position of the node. pose: the root position, canonicalised under the board's exact symmetries (x-flip, y-flip and their product -- the side arcs sit at cx = ±66.667, cy = 0 -- reflections negate dir and reverse foot order) and each tripod's 120-degree relabelling (rot mod 2π/3 with feet renumbered); `sym` stores the transform back.
- kind = "point" (even plies): region = { q : d_a(q,pose) <= eps_a and d_v(q,pose) <= eps_v }. eps = [0,0] until a star has been run.
- kind = "arc" (odd plies): arc = { pv, dir, span:[s0,s1] rad, step, perSample:[{s, limitSig:{reason,foot,line}, minGap, legal}] }, child = id of the plies-1 point it lands on. Position at s: pose with pose[side] replaced by moverAt(pose[side], pv, -dir, s). EVERY arc position plays (pv,dir) to stop s and lands exactly on child. Region = union over s in span of Ball(arcPose(s), eps).
- margin, lip, need: as in the existing certificates. plies 2: margin = W = worst dead margin (u past the rim) from verifyAllReplies; plies >= 4: margin = min over the node's samples of (child W - need); lip = the certified constant (>= 1), need = lip·gapU/2.
- samples (even plies >= 4, the constructive evidence): per victim arm: [{ t1, landing[6] (mover by moverAt, pushed piece from the reply record), replySig:{pairs,onset}, choice:{pvA, dirA, sigma, moveSig}, target: nodeId | "THROW", W, lip, need }] -- the attacker's certified move at every victim stop and the dead child it lands on.
- star: null | { h, probes:[12 × {axis, sign, status, W, profileOK}], lip6 } -- the transverse measurement; the only place transverse thickness comes from.
- engine: { trials, agree } from the engine check of the claim.

Membership: lookup(q, plies, side) canonicalises q, then for point nodes tests the two balls, for arc nodes takes min over the arc's samples of d(q, arcPose(s)) (chord error <= R·step²/8, negligible at 1°) and returns depth = eps - d (componentwise min). A level-1 "throw region" is never stored: membership is computed by six sweeps (the existing bestThrow).

**Algorithm.** Level k+1 is two half-steps: an even (LOST) point is grown into odd (WIN) arcs by unwinding, and odd arcs are grown into the next even point by CONSTRUCTION -- the region a victim family must land in is built ON that family's own landing points, sample by sample, so containment never has to hit a pre-existing thin region by luck.

Step 0 -- swingLimitMemo. Wrap swingLimit in a Map keyed on (x, y, rot rounded to 1e-7, pv, dir). Measured here: throwMargin = 134 ms of which the engine's simMoveToLimit is 98 ms and the law sweep 36 ms; verifyAllReplies recomputes the attacker's six limits at every reply stop although the attacker does not move along a contact-free reply. Nothing else changes; verifyAllReplies goes from the measured 60 s to ~20 s.

Step 1 -- seeds. The four dead points become plies-2 point nodes via deadCertificate(pieces, victim) = verifyAllReplies(pieces, victim, REPLICA, 2, 3, 1) + deadProfile (per victim arm, the sequence of certifying attacker arcs over t2 with their sig.pairs) + simCheckDead. W and the profile are stored.

Step 2 -- transverse thickness of a point: certifyStar(node, h = 0.5). Twelve probes at ±h on each of the six axes (rotations ±h/R), each probe re-running the node's own certificate (deadCertificate at plies 2; certifyLostPoint at plies >= 4, so it is run lazily there). Refuse unless all 13 carry the node's verdict and their profiles are consistent (same arc sequences per arm, boundaries shifted by at most one gap). lip6 = max(1, 3·max over axes |W(+h) - W(-h)|/(2h)); eps_a = eps_v = min(h, W_centre/lip6). This is the joint replacement for certifyDeadBox: 13 samples in 6-D instead of 27 in 3-D.

Step 3 -- LOST(2j) point -> WIN(2j+1) arcs: unwindArcs(node). The --back-from loop as it stands (moverAt with -dir over 2..170° at 1°, anyOff and the hub bound, then the engine plays the forward swing with load/pinFoot/applySwing and both pieces must land on the child within 0.05u/1e-3 rad), plus per sample: swing(prev, att, pv, dir, s, {record, trace}) to get minGap (add to swing: the minimum over steps of the closest tube distance, one line) and the signature, and limitSig = the engine's g.limitReason/foot/line at simMoveToLimit. Split into separate arc nodes wherever limitSig changes or minGap <= MIND (contact-free arrivals only, as now). Radii: eps_a = min(child.eps_a/3, (minGap - MIND)/4, limitSlack), eps_v = child.eps_v; limitSlack from 12 swingLimit-only probes at ±eps_a (98 ms each) requiring limit >= s with the same limitSig. Engine check: random q in the tube, play (pv,dir) to s, landing inside the child's balls, then simCheckDead at the landing.

Step 4 -- WIN(2j+1) arcs -> LOST(2j+2) point: certifyLostPoint(P, victim, plies).
 a. Candidates P: the game rows i-2 (two rows before each mined dead row; victim to move, the row --mine --back 1 already reads); later unwindTwice: a WIN-arc position with the victim's played arm unwound contact-free. Pre-screen with escapeScreen (6° victim stops, hinted attacker arm, deadScreen only; ~18 min) before spending hours.
 b. For each victim arm: fam = replyFamily(P, victim, pv, dir, K) (reused as is). fam.out.maxFootR > EDGE => the reply throws the attacker: ESCAPE. Stops t1 every 2° as prefixes of the record; landing L(t1) = [moverAt(P[victim], pv, dir, t1), record(t1)] -- the family carries the pushed attacker pose exactly as verifyAllReplies does.
 c. Per stop: findWin(L, plies-1, hint). (i) lookup(L, plies-1, attacker): a hit with depth > 0 on an existing WIN node makes an edge to it (this fires near the played stop, where L sits on the seed's unwound arc). (ii) Otherwise extendDead(L, hint), hint = the previous stop's choice (pvA, dirA, sigma): ONE swing with record along (pvA, dirA) from L gives every sigma at once (if it throws the victim at some sigma the target is THROW, plies 1); screen sigma in {sigma-2°, sigma, sigma+2°} with deadScreen = verifyAllReplies with {screen:true} (8° stops, no gap certification, returns the min sampled best margin, ~5 s); run deadCertificate on the best; dead => a new plies-(plies-2) point node D(t1) and an edge; else widen: the whole arm at 6° screened, then the other five arms; nothing => certifyForcedIn2(L, attacker): refuted on every candidate => ESCAPE-in-(plies/2+1) with the victim stop as the checkable claim, else UNRESOLVED.

Step 5 -- run step 3 on every new LOST(4) point => WIN(5) arcs (minutes). Level k+1 from level k = step 4 then step 3 on level k's points. Reuse (edges to existing nodes) happens only at 4c(i); a hit requires the target's eps > 0, so certifyStar is run lazily, once, on any node a lookup lands within h = 0.5u of.

**Soundness.** Relative to the engine, three facts and two arguments, all inherited from what exists.
Facts: (1) the law in REPLICA settings IS the engine (0.000u/0.000deg on 200 events), so every recorded sweep is the engine's trajectory and a stop at t is a prefix of it; (2) swing limits are always the engine's simMoveToLimit, never the law's -- the crossing budget and rim are the engine's path-dependent rule and are not modelled; (3) unwound arcs are played forward by the engine to an exact landing, and every certified claim is followed by an engine check (simCheckDead on balls, forward play on arcs, simCheckLost on the policy). A contradiction is a bug.
Argument A (Lipschitz coverage along a one-parameter family; this is the whole of verifyAllReplies' gap logic, unchanged): between neighbouring stops the position moves at most 2R·Δ, the margin's slope is bounded by 3× the observed finite difference with floor 1, and both ends must clear lip·gapU/2; failures subdivide to the engine's substep. What is new is only WHAT the margin is. At plies 2 it is the throw margin in u past the rim. At plies 4 it is the child's dead margin W(t1) along the constructed chain, and W is a continuous function of t1 exactly when the chain is: the victim reply is a prefix of one sweep (continuous by construction); the attacker move (pvA, dirA, sigma(t1)) with sigma interpolated is continuous in t1 provided its contact history does not change -- which is the moveSig equality demanded across every gap; the child's dead verdict varies continuously provided ITS contact histories do not change -- the profile-consistency demand. Grazing surfaces enter exactly here, as they do in certifyThrowBox: a change of leg pairs or a jump of first-touch onset between neighbours refuses the gap, and the sliver acceptance is not used above the throw level, so a gap that cannot be certified at the substep is UNRESOLVED, never dead.
Argument B (exact landing plus a derived, not sampled, perturbation bound for arcs): moverAt(moverAt(p,pv,-dir,s),pv,dir,s) = p identically, so every arc position lands on its child exactly, not approximately, and a stop short of s from a shorter unwinding sweeps a sub-arc of the same contact-free arc. Perturbing an arc position by δ moves the landing by at most 3·|δ| on the mover's own component (|δxy| + 2R·δrot: the pivot foot shifts and the lever rotates) and by 0 on the other piece's -- the pushed piece is not touched (contact-free). Contact-freeness survives the perturbation while minGap - MIND >= 4·eps (the tube gap changes by at most the relative displacement); legality is checked at 12 limit probes with the same limitSig (the limit is discontinuous in the mover's pose where a foot's arc becomes tangent to a printed line; a changed limitSig refuses the tube). Component-wise radii are why a plies-4 ball has radius eps2/3 and not eps2/9: the victim's reply amplifies only δv (by 3), the attacker's move only δa (by 3), so the dead child moves by at most 3|δ| in the max-metric. Where a reply or a move pushes, the amplification is not 3 but the measured finite difference of the landing over the record (× 3, floor 1) and the contact signatures must match -- the same standard, just measured instead of derived.
Transverse thickness of points is never assumed: it comes only from the star -- twelve probes carrying the node's verdict with consistent profiles, slope × 3, floor 1. That is the same evidential standard as certifyThrowBox/certifyDeadBox (whose 27-grid also only samples slopes, and certifies the cell diagonal by the same allowance), now taken in the joint space.
Engine-vs-law slack is inherited: certification is in the engine's own metric; escape claims keep ESC_TOL = 0.75u; an ESCAPE at plies >= 4 is only claimed when certifyForcedIn2 refutes every attacker candidate, each refutation itself an ESC_TOL-guarded escape. Symmetry canonicalisation uses only exact isometries of the printed board (the D2 group of x/y reflections; rings and rim are invariant under everything, the two lens arcs are swapped or fixed by the reflections) and exact tripod relabellings; reflections reverse dir and foot order, which the canonicaliser applies to arms, not just poses.

**Cost.** Measured on this machine now (load 9.6 on 4 cores; everything ~2.4× the quiet-machine figures in the brief): throwMargin 134 ms = swingLimit (engine) 98 ms + law sweep 36 ms; verifyAllReplies on seed #2 (W = 2.19u) 60.3 s. With swingLimitMemo: ~20 s per dead certificate (the 540-odd throw sweeps drop from 134 to 36 ms each; the six attacker limits are computed once). deadScreen ~5 s.

Stage B, joint thickening of the existing 11 win-in-2 arcs (~500 degrees of arc at 1° = 500 samples): per sample one engine forward play (~100 ms) + one law sweep with minGap (36 ms) + 12 limit probes (12 × 98 ms = 1.2 s) ≈ 1.4 s -> 500 × 1.4 s ≈ 12 core-min.
Stage C, one dead-in-3 candidate P4 (a game row i-2): 6 victim arms × ~40 stops (2° with the subdivisions the ~1u margins will force) = 240 landings. **Risks.** 1. Regions stay thin: radii divide by about 3 per LOST level (eps2 ≈ 0.4-0.5u from a star on a ~2u margin -> eps4 ≈ 0.15u), so lookup reuse will rarely fire beyond the played line; the graph is a certified tree of lines with tubes, not fat regions. Fat regions need fat margins (deeper throws): choose seeds by W, and expect the 0.85u seed (l5807vazg-0) to fail the star at h = 0.5.
2. Cost is exponential in LOST levels: a dead-in-3 point ≈ 240 dead certificates (5 core-hours busy); a dead-in-4 point ≈ 240 dead-in-3 points ≈ 1200 core-hours. Level 3 is the frontier on this machine unless reuse hits; do not plan level 4 without a different idea (larger margins, or relative-frame sweep caching that only helps if the same relative poses recur).
3. Along-t1 gaps will often need subdivision to 1° or below: with margins ~1u the 2° allowance (need >= 0.81u at lip 1) is barely met; budget 40-80 stops per arm, not 35, and expect UNRESOLVED gaps where the attacker's winning move changes arm (no sliver acceptance above the throw level).
4. Swing-limit discontinuities: the crossing budget is path-dependent (episodes, start-line grace armed by pinFoot, cornerEps); a sub-arc of a legal arc is assumed legal and the 12 limit probes assume the limit's discontinuity set is crossed only with a change of limitSig. Both must be falsified empirically first (forward-play random tube points in the engine, as step 3's check does).
5. The Lipschitz constants are 3× observed finite differences, floor 1 -- a heuristic, exactly as strong as the existing certificates and no stronger; at level 3 it is composed three deep (throw margin, dead margin along t1, star slopes), so the engine checks are load-bearing, not decoration. A star's 12 probes sample slopes more coarsely than a 27-grid.

7. The candidate P4's may simply not be dead-in-3 (the loser may have had an escape two moves out); an ESCAPE verdict costs certifyForcedIn2 refutations (~10 min per escaping stop) and most failures will end UNRESOLVED, which proves nothing. Run escapeScreen first.
8. Machine load: measured at load 9.6 on 4 cores; four certification lanes will fight the trainer; the wall-clock estimates assume they get ~40% of a core each.
9. Symmetry canonicalisation is easy to get subtly wrong (reflections flip dir and reverse foot order; the tripod relabelling permutes pv). Test it by certifying a reflected seed and demanding the same W and profile before any lookup relies on it.
