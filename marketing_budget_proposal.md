# Tau launch budget: A$100, A$500, A$1000 and A$2000

**Plan:** make a few physical sets as cheaply as possible, run the AI challenge, promote it with short-form videos and one long YouTube video, get the Steam page up, and send sets to a few martial artists.

**The challenge**
- **Beat level 13:** you get a free Steam key.
- **Be the first to beat level 14 as Red:** you get a physical set.

All figures are in AUD and come from the two cost models in the repo:
- `tau_cost_model_v5.html`: firm quotes from July 2026 for the disc, pieces, magnets, stickers and postage.
- `physical/tau_cost_model_v2.1.html`: the cheaper options (home-printed pieces, QR rules card, print-on-demand boxes, card inserts).

Anything marked *est.* is an estimate in those models or mine. Re-check prices at order time, because the disc quote was only valid for 30 days.

## What is free

- **Steam keys.** Keys generated in Steamworks cost nothing, and you've already paid the Steam Direct fee (App ID 5353480). Valve allows keys for giveaways but asks that you request a reasonable number, so ask for a batch of about 100.
- **Steam page and Next Fest.** Setting these up costs only your time.
- **Video tools:**
  - the rotoscope tool
  - ad variations and pairing (menu options 6 and 7)
  - AI-GAMES.bat
  - Director mode
  - OBS for recording
  - CapCut for music and captions
- **Organic posting** on Reddit, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram and BGG.
- **Box design.** `physical/make_box.py` already produces the dielines (`tau_box_dieline.svg`, flat-pack 267 and 400, insert tray).

**Avoid at this stage:** a custom round box carries about A$200 in tooling and needs 100 to 500 units (v2.1), and a moulded pulp insert is A$1200 in tooling. Both are for a later 100+ run.

**Home-printed pieces:** v2.1 warns these are fragile at the ankles. Fillet the joints, widen the foot pads, and avoid silk PLA or resin. They're fine for prizes and gifts.

**Your proven Taiwan route:** 430 steel with UV print from a Taiwan shop is A$38 per disc (v2.1). It's dearer, but family can collect the discs and check them. Jiangy is the cheap option.

**Not in the model:** shipping the parts to you. Ten discs and the pieces sent from China will probably cost **A$100–150** *est.* Ask Jiangy and the printer for a shipping quote with the order.

**Posting a finished set** (about 1.5 kg):
- about A$64 to the US, UK or Europe (model figure)
- about A$15–25 within Australia *est.*

Canada (Firas Zahabi) is probably close to the US price.

## What I'd recommend

**Start at A$500 and grow it to A$1000 if the challenge takes off.** The prize set and the gift sets get the attention. Ads are the weakest use of money at this stage, so only boost videos that are already working.

## Ordering (lead times are long)

1. **Before announcing the challenge**, order the discs, pieces and magnets. Magnets take about a month.
2. **While the parts are on their way**, put the Steam page up, post short videos made with the tools, and record the long video.
3. **When the sets arrive**, finish them and photograph them for the sell sheet and the Steam page. Then announce the challenge, posting the video and Reddit thread together.
4. **Gift sets** go to the creators' gyms with a handwritten note and the URL card. Warm them up first by commenting on their content for a week or two, and keep the message to four sentences with no ask (template in `docs/handover/04_gtm_playbook.md`). Mighty Mouse streams games, so his management or Twitch business email is the better route than a cold DM.

## Challenge rules to settle

- **Level 13, Steam key:** one key per person. The win is proven with the replay link. Close it after the first 100 keys, or on a date, whichever comes first. Since keys are free, the two-tab trick (copying the AI's moves from a second tab) only costs you keys, not money.
- **Level 14 as Red, physical set:** first verified win only. The winner may be asked to beat it again on a video call. Decide which countries you'll ship to, and set a closing date.
- **Victorian promotion rules:** both prizes are skill-based, which usually needs no permit, but check before posting.
- **Copy and videos:** keep the level 12–14 nets out of the videos (AI-GAMES.bat already does this). Also keep any "Blue wins every high-level game" pattern out of the copy.

## Open numbers

- **Shipping parts to you:** get a real quote.
- **UV print price on brushed 430:** the model's one open board number. Order a sample first.
- **Disc quote currency:** confirm whether Jiangy's A$4.75 is AUD. An earlier note had US$3.87 at 500 units.
- **Box cost:** print-on-demand boxes cost about A$6 each in v2.1, but check one fits a 267 mm disc. A standard size may need to be square.
