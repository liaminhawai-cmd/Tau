# Ads and videos

## Tools (menu options 6 and 7 in Tau-Marketing.bat)

- **6. Ad variations.** Give it a `.corrected.json` from the editor. It renders every look (clean, ghost trails, neon, ink on white, figures on a Tau ring), in phone and widescreen, plain and mirrored: 20 clips into `<name>_variants\`. Give it the second the throw lands and that moment gets slow motion and a short hold.
- **7. Pair a throw with a Tau clip.** Stacked for phones, side by side for widescreen, or one after the other. An end card with the logo goes on the end.
- For Tau footage, open `tau-game.com/#director`, tick **Hide UI**, and record with OBS. The Cinematic Studio (`studio.html`) also works.
- When pairing, render the throw without a title (`--title` empty, which is the default), because the pair tool adds its own labels.
- Output has no sound. Add music in CapCut or the app you post from, which also gives you its licensed music library.

Command-line use, for more control: `python ads\animate.py --help` and `python ads\compose.py --help`.

## The challenge

**Play as Red.** Red moves second against the full-strength level 13, which is the harder clear, and it's the same opponent on the web and on Steam, so anyone can enter for free.

**Cheating.** Someone could open a second tab or window, play level 13 against itself there, and copy its moves into the challenge game. That is a mirror match of the same AI, so it isn't a guaranteed win, but it is a real coin flip. Asking for the replay doesn't detect it, because the moves look like strong play. What helps:
- The rules say no computer help, and a winner may be asked to beat level 13 again live, on a video call or a stream. Someone copying moves will struggle to do it twice while being watched.
- Optional later: the game could record time per move in the replay. Copied moves have an even, delayed rhythm. Not needed for launch.

## Ad series (15 to 30 seconds each, phone first)

Each one is a throw paired with a Tau position that uses the same idea, then the end card "Beat level 13. Win a steel set."

1. **Kuzushi.** A judo off-balance (de-ashi-barai or similar), then a Tau move where a swing leaves the opponent with a foot near the rim and no line left to cross back.
2. **The edge.** A sumo push to the tawara, then a Tau piece driven to the edge that pivots and escapes.
3. **Reversal.** A sumo utchari (thrown at the edge by the one being pushed), then a Tau counter from the rim.
4. **The challenge itself.** No fight footage: a level 13 game at speed with the line "Nobody has beaten it yet."

You pick the throws and the Tau moments. I can find matching Tau positions in saved replays if you send me the links.
