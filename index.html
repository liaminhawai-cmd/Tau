TAU

Tau is a physics-based abstract strategy board game, sometimes compared to Abalone or Kabuto Sumo. Each player controls one three-legged piece. On your turn, pin one foot in place and swing the other two around it in a continuous arc, not a jump between grid squares. Cross a line to close in on your opponent, but you may only cross one line per turn. Push their piece past the edge of the round board to win. Play against the computer across nine difficulty levels, locally against a friend, or online against another player. Free to play in your browser.
